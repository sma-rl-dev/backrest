# Workshop layout and machine notes

## Zones

- Bench room: assembly bench, glue-up table, finish cabinet.
- Machine room: table saw, planer, bandsaw, dust extractor.
- Loading bay: timber rack, offcut bins, delivery door.

## Machine notes

- Table saw: 250 mm blade, riving knife stays fitted for all ripping.
- Planer: check the feed rollers after every 20 hours of use.
- Dust extractor: filters cleaned on the first Friday of the month; the
  spare cartridge lives under the loading bench.

## Storage

Sheet goods stand on the north wall, solid stock on the rack by the bay.
Offcuts under 300 mm go to the kindling bin, not back on the rack.
