# Oak dining table

Client: Mercer Street cafe
Commissioned: 2026-09-02
Handover: 2026-11-06

2.4 m refectory table in reclaimed white oak for the back room, seating ten.
Legs are trestle-style with a through-wedge tenon; top is 44 mm thick.

## Notes

- Reclaimed boards arrived with old paint on two edges; planed back 12 mm.
- Client wants visible knots, no filler on the top face.
- Delivery access is through the courtyard, double doors 1.4 m wide.
