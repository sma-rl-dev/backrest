# Front room shelving

Client: Harlow family, 14 Fern Lane
Commissioned: 2026-05-28
Handover: 2026-07-18

Three-bay open shelving in white oak, wall-fixed, with a 30 mm face frame.
The left bay is deepened to 380 mm for the record player.

## Notes

- Wall anchors rated for 40 kg per bay; the plaster is 1930s lath and needs
  the longer anchor sleeves.
- Finish is Osmo hard wax oil, two coats, 24 hours between coats.
- Client asked for the uprights to stop 40 mm below the ceiling.
