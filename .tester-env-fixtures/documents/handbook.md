# Field Handbook

Lakeside Workshop keeps this handbook in the shared documents folder.

## Opening checklist

1. Unlock the side door and disarm the alarm.
2. Check the workshop log for overnight notes.
3. Turn on the compressor and let it warm up for five minutes.
4. Confirm the dust extractor gate is open before sanding.

## Closing checklist

1. Sweep the bench and the floor around the table saw.
2. Return clamps to the rack and recharge the drivers.
3. Empty the dust bin into the skip outside the loading door.
4. Lock the finish cabinet and arm the alarm.
